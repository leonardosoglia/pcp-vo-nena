# Abstract

---

This work describes the development and implementation of a digital Production Planning and Control (PPC) system for a Brazilian semi-industrial confectionery, replacing the manual control previously kept on paper sheets. The system began with the digitalization of the daily production sheet and evolved into a PPC integrated with the company's ERP (SIGE Cloud) in read-only mode, in which the Production Order acts as the bridge between the ERP's accounting truth and the factory's operational truth, while keeping the production decision human. Functionalities classically restricted to large industries were implemented at a small scale: material requirements explosion (simplified MRP), ABC analysis and target calibration through moving averages. The automatic cutting and production suggestions showed an adherence to the actual decisions of Management of approximately 85% for the straw (*palha*) products and 50% to 70% for the coconut candy (*cocada*). The system was extended to weight-based production costing, channel margin analysis, real sales reading and per-product contribution calculation, raising cost coverage from 17% to 64% of sales. The matrix-material margin trap and the limits of the ERP regarding conversion cost are discussed. The work contributes a replicable case of PPC digitalization in a small food industry.

**Keywords:** Production Planning and Control. Systems integration. Production cost. Contribution margin. ABC analysis.
